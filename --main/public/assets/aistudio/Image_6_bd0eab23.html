The Genesis • Saad 8.5

“On September 28, 2014, my original life ended. Doctors told my family there was less than a five percent chance I would survive the night.”

Before that catastrophic midnight collision, Saad Hamed was an invincible Marine veteran nicknamed Teflon—a man forged in the demanding crucible of the 510 Airframes Unit. But physical strength could not insulate him from the devastating blow of a severe Traumatic Brain Injury, shattered facial bones, and months trapped in a coma.

Emerging into a disorienting, reconstructed world, Saad lost his corporate career, physical equilibrium, and familiar cognitive rhythms. Yet, rooted in an unyielding faith and his Marine ethos, he christened himself Saad 8.5: an updated, battle-tested version of the human spirit who now lives for a singular daily assignment:
