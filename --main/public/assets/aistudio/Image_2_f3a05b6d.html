Changed My Life

“The true measure of a man is not how hard he falls, but how fiercely he rises in the presence of grace.”

On September 28, 2014, a devastating motorcycle collision left Saad Hamed with a mere 5% chance of survival. Against all medical odds, guided by unwavering faith and international prayers echoing across three Abrahamic faiths, he emerged from the edge of oblivion to forge a definitive blueprint for human resilience.

From months in a medically induced coma through the excruciating labyrinth of traumatic brain injury rehabilitation, Saad refused to let clinical verdicts define his horizon. 928 takes readers straight into the trauma intensive care room, through raw hospital corridors, and deep into the sacred interior wrestling of a spirit that refused to surrender.

It is simultaneously a harrowing survival memoir, a tender tribute to family sacrifice, and an actionable spiritual compass for anyone standing before their own insurmountable storm.
